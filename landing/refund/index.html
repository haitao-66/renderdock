Refund Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Subscriptions

If you are not satisfied with your first paid month, email hello@renderdock.dev within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. This applies to the first payment only.

2. Renewals

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal; cancellation stops future renewals. Payments for months that have already begun are not refunded after the 14-day window.

3. Overage charges

Overage charges cover renders that have already been delivered and are non-refundable.

4. Beta period

While the Service is in beta, the free tier is available without payment, so there is nothing to refund. If you are ever charged in error, contact us and the charge will be reversed.

5. How refunds work

Send your account email and the reason for the request to hello@renderdock.dev. Approved refunds are issued through Paddle to your original payment method, typically within 14 days.
