Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account information: the email address you register with and, when you subscribe, billing details collected by Paddle (see section 4).
  • Usage data: API key, request timestamps, and render counts, used for quotas, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.
  • Waitlist signups: the email address you submit on our site.
  • Correspondence: emails you send us.

2. Content you render

Content you submit through the API (Markdown, HTML, URLs, templates, and data) is processed transiently to produce your output files. It is not retained after the render completes. Rendered files are delivered directly to you.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers on our website.
  • Our website does not set cookies at this time.

4. Payment processing

Payments are handled by Paddle, the merchant of record. When you subscribe, your payment details are collected and processed by Paddle under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number.

5. Logs and security

Server logs are kept for security monitoring and debugging and are rotated. Logs may include IP addresses and request metadata.

6. Retention and your rights

Account and usage data is kept while your account is active. You may request export or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing hello@renderdock.dev. Waitlist emails are deleted upon request.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
